## Executive Overview

The **Customer & Region Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Excel-based analytics project focused on understanding customer distribution, regional sales performance, profitability, and sales trends.

The dashboard transforms transactional sales data into a business-facing view that helps identify where sales are concentrated, which customer segments contribute most to performance, and how sales evolve over time.

### Core Analytical Scope

- Customer and order distribution
- Regional sales and profit performance
- Customer segment contribution
- Monthly sales trends
- State-level geographic distribution
- Category, year, and quarter filtering

---

## Business Problem & Key Findings

The analysis addresses several practical business questions:

1. **Regional Performance:** Which regions generate the strongest sales and profit?
2. **Customer Segmentation:** Which customer segment contributes the largest share of sales and orders?
3. **Geographic Concentration:** Which cities and states contain the largest customer and order concentrations?
4. **Seasonality:** How does customer and sales activity change throughout the year?

### Key Findings

- The **West region** leads in total sales.
- The **Consumer segment** contributes the largest share of sales and orders, at roughly **50%+**.
- Sales trend upward toward the end of the year, with **November** representing the observed peak.
- **New York** and **Los Angeles** are among the leading cities for customers and orders.
